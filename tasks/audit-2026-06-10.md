### A.1 CRITICAL — Elevation gain: two code paths, both numerically wrong, in opposite directions

**Files:** `repositories/routesRepo.ts:276-295` (`calcElevationGainFromPoints`), `app/ride-summary.tsx:90-117`, `lib/elevationCorrection.ts:91-153`.

**Path divergence (confirmed):**
1. Summary screen: `calcElevationGainFromPoints(activePoints)` where `activePoints` = DEM-corrected points, **or raw points** if `correctElevations()` fails OR the 5-second `Promise.race` fallback wins (`ride-summary.tsx:92-96`).
2. Upload: `uploadRecordedRide` is called with **raw `points`** (`ride-summary.tsx:151`) and recomputes gain internally (`routesRepo.ts:327`) — **DEM correction is display-only, never persisted**. The on-screen number and the number every other user sees on the route card are computed from different inputs.
3. SavedRide stores no elevation at all — the locally saved ride loses the number the user just saw.
4. Route detail recomputes climb profiles from the stored GPX, whose `<ele>` values are raw GPS (`gpxGenerator.ts` writes raw `p.alt`), so the profile shape differs from the one shown on the summary.

**The 5 s fallback races a function that sleeps by design:** `correctElevations` downsamples to 1 pt/50 m and fetches batches of 100 with a mandatory 1.1 s sleep between batches (`elevationCorrection.ts:107`). A ride of D km produces ~20·D samples:
- ≥ ~5 km → 2 batches → ≥1.1 s of sleep + 2 fetches
- ≥ ~25 km → ≥5 batches → ≥4.4 s of sleep alone → **the 5 s raw fallback wins essentially always**
So for typical road rides, even the summary shows raw-GPS gain. The spinner stops, a number renders, and **nothing signals which path ran**. The late DEM result is discarded (race already resolved).

**The algorithm is wrong on both inputs.** `MIN_DELTA = 1` is applied **per consecutive point pair** after a 7-point moving average:
- **Raw input (the common case):** residual vertical noise after the 7-pt MA is σ ≈ 1–4 m; every positive excursion ≥ 1 m accumulates. Over thousands of points (4 s / 15 m apart) this adds **hundreds of meters of phantom D+** on a flat ride — matches the #1 user complaint.
- **DEM input (the "good" case):** corrected altitudes are linearly interpolated between 50 m samples, so consecutive deltas are smooth: Δ ≈ grade × ~15 m step. Any grade below ~6.7% gives Δ < 1 m → **contributes zero**. A steady 4–5% climb reports near-0 m gain. The threshold-per-step design undercounts smooth data and overcounts noisy data — users see inconsistent numbers in both directions.

**User-visible impact:** flat 40 km ride shows e.g. 600 m D+; the same ride re-recorded on a short route with DEM success shows ~0 m on a real 300 m climb day; the uploaded route card shows a third number.

**Proposed fix (single source of truth, see Part C):** compute gain **once** in a `finalizeRide()` step from a canonical processed track; use a **hysteresis algorithm** (accumulate climb between local extrema, count only climbs ≥ threshold — e.g. 2 m for DEM-corrected, 5 m for raw); `await` DEM correction in the save path with a sane timeout and **persist `elevationGainM` + `elevationCorrected: boolean`** on both SavedRide and the uploaded row; never recompute downstream.

### A.2 CRITICAL — Actual vs estimated time crossed on every catalog surface and on the share card

**Files:** `components/RouteCard.tsx:89`, `components/RouteListItem.tsx:111`, `app/route/[id].tsx:227`, `components/climbs/ClimbListItem.tsx:102`, `app/time/[duration].tsx:50`, `components/share/ShareCard.tsx:121-129`, `repositories/routesRepo.ts:205-231, 347`.

- `uploadRecordedRide` stores the rider's **actual moving time** as `duration_minutes` (`routesRepo.ts:347`).
- But every card and the route-detail stats strip display `calculateRideMinutes(distanceKm, elevationM, riderLevel)` — a flat-equivalent estimate at 26/29/33 km/h (`utils/rideTimeCalculator.ts`). A user who rode 2 h sees their own uploaded route claim ~1 h 10 m; **the number also changes per viewer** (rider level) and is inflated by the phantom elevation from A.1 (estimate adds elevation/100 km-equivalents).
- `ShareCard` receives `durationSeconds` in its Props but **never destructures it** (`ShareCard.tsx:121`); for regular routes it renders `predictedTime` at hardcoded `'intermediate'` (`:128-129`) under the label "ČAS VOŽNJE". **The post-ride story card shows a prediction, not the ride the user just finished.**
- `getRoutesByDuration` filters on stored `duration_minutes` while cards display the estimate (`routesRepo.ts:205-231`) → a route can sit in the "1h" bucket while its card says "2h".
- `route/[id].tsx:337` passes the real `durationMinutes * 60` to the share sheet while its own stats strip at `:227` shows the estimate — two different times on one screen.

**Proposed fix:** distinguish recorded vs curated routes (e.g. `created_by` set or a `is_recorded` flag). Recorded routes display their stored `duration_minutes` everywhere; the estimate is used only when no recorded time exists (curated routes / `duration_minutes = 0`). ShareCard must render the `durationSeconds` prop when provided.

### A.3 HIGH — Moving time: >30 s GPS gaps silently deleted; saved "duration" is moving time

**Files:** `lib/rideRecorder.ts:48, 69-76`, `app/ride-summary.tsx:62-65, 139`.

- `computeMovingSeconds` sums only inter-point gaps < 30 s. A GPS dropout > 30 s **while riding** (tunnel, forest, OS throttling) deletes that time entirely, while the distance across the gap **is** still counted (haversine between the bracketing points) → saved time shrinks and **avg speed inflates** — exactly the "numbers too good to be true" complaint.
- Conversely a 25 s standstill counts fully as moving (quantization in the other direction).
- `durSec` silently falls back to `elapsedSeconds` when `movingSeconds === 0` (`ride-summary.tsx:64`) — a hidden third semantic.
- Elapsed time is computed but **displayed nowhere and never persisted**; users can't see ride duration incl. breaks at all.

**Proposed fix:** in `finalizeRide()`: per-gap clamp — count `min(dt, 30 s)` for gaps where implied speed (gap distance/dt) ≥ ~0.8 m/s, count `dt` fully for normal gaps, skip gaps with near-zero implied speed. Persist **both** `movingSeconds` and `elapsedSeconds`; show "moving time" labelled as such, with elapsed available on the summary/detail.

### A.3b MED — Cockpit timer only advances when GPS points arrive

`recording.tsx:350` renders `movingSeconds`, which is recomputed only on accepted points (`rideRecorder.ts:152`); the 1 s tick updates only `elapsedSeconds` (`:186-190`, not displayed). The big timer stutters in ~4 s jumps and freezes during GPS gaps, then jumps forward. Fix: derive display time on tick (last moving value + time since last point if below gap threshold), or show elapsed on the cockpit.

### A.4 HIGH — Distance filter: stationary drift admitted, slow movement path-straightened

**File:** `lib/rideRecorder.ts:45-47, 105-116`.

- `MAX_ACCURACY_M = 40` admits poor fixes; while stopped at a light/café, drift steps > 3 m pass the jitter filter (plausible speed, dist ≥ 3 m) and **inflate distance as a random walk**. With auto-pause also suppressing time, avg speed inflates twice.
- The jitter filter (`dist < 3 m && dt < 5 s`) rejects sub-3 m moves relative to the **last accepted point**; on slow climbs (~2-3 km/h) every other fix is dropped. Distance mostly survives (the next accepted point carries the accumulated displacement) but the track is straightened — minor undercount that partially offsets drift noise. Unpredictable either way.

**Proposed fix:** speed-aware acceptance: reject points whose implied speed < ~0.5 m/s **and** displacement < max(5 m, accuracy/2) — i.e., treat "not credibly moving" as stationary regardless of accuracy; tighten accuracy gate for distance accumulation (e.g. don't accumulate distance from fixes with accuracy > 25 m, while still keeping them for the track).

### A.5 MED — Recovered rides compute elapsed differently from normal stops

`hydrateFromActiveRecording` sets `elapsedSeconds = lastPointTs − startedAt` (`rideRecorder.ts:301-302`); a normal stop uses `Date.now() − startTimestamp` (`:252`). Recovered rides exclude trailing time after the last fix (arguably more correct!) while normal stops include time spent staring at the confirm dialog. Whichever semantic is chosen, it should be one function. Impact today is limited because elapsed is only used via the `durSec` fallback, but it becomes load-bearing once elapsed is persisted (A.3).

### A.6 MED — Elevation loading UX lies about which number you got

`ride-summary.tsx:268-273` shows a spinner for ≤5 s, then a green number — identical presentation whether the value came from DEM or raw fallback. The late-arriving DEM result is thrown away. Fix folds into A.1: keep correcting in the background, update the number when correction lands (pre-save), and persist the corrected value with `elevationCorrected` flag; optionally a subtle "(GPS estimate)" marker when uncorrected.

### A.7 MED — `submitRoute` inserts visible all-zero routes

`routesRepo.ts:246-264` inserts `distance_km: 0, elevation_m: 0, duration_minutes: 0`, and `listRoutes()` (`:115-127`) returns **all** rows with no review filter — a user-submitted GPX route appears in the catalog immediately with 0 km / 0 m / estimate-from-zero time. Fix: parse the GPX client-side for distance/elevation at submit time, or add a `status='pending'` column filtered out of all list queries (the "(pending admin review)" comment suggests the latter was intended).

## PART C — Proposed single-source-of-truth design: `finalizeRide()`

New module `lib/rideMetrics.ts`, pure and unit-testable:

```ts
interface RideMetrics {
  distanceMeters:     number;
  elapsedSeconds:     number;   // stop − start, one definition for normal + recovered
  movingSeconds:      number;   // speed-aware, gap-clamped
  avgSpeedKmh:        number;   // distance / moving
  elevationGainM:     number;   // hysteresis algorithm
  elevationCorrected: boolean;  // DEM succeeded?
  points:             RecordedPoint[]; // canonical processed track (DEM alts when corrected)
  elevationProfile?:  number[];
}

async function finalizeRide(raw: RecordedPoint[], startTs: number, stopTs: number): Promise<RideMetrics>
```

Flow: clean track once → `await correctElevations` (no 5 s race; honest timeout ~20 s, the save button can show "correcting…") → compute **all** metrics from the processed track → that one object is (a) shown on the summary, (b) persisted on SavedRide (add `elevationGainM`, `elevationCorrected`, `elapsedSeconds`, `avgSpeedKmh`), (c) passed into `uploadRecordedRide`, which **stops recomputing anything**, and (d) used for GPX generation (corrected `<ele>`). Display rule: recorded routes show recorded time; `calculateRideMinutes` only where no recorded time exists. Gain algorithm: hysteresis between local extrema, threshold 2 m (corrected) / 5 m (raw).

**First unit tests** (pure functions, ideal targets): `calcElevationGain` (flat-noisy fixture → ~0; steady 5% DEM fixture → true gain; known GPX → Strava-comparable), `computeMovingSeconds` (dropout/pause fixtures), `computeDistanceMeters`, `downsample`/interpolation in `elevationCorrection`, `computeElevationProfileFromPoints`. Fixture: one known GPX in `__tests__/fixtures/`.
